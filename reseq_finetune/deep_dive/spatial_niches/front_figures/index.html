Hypoxia is a front, not an island

Velocity-defined entering and exiting tumor cells form a spatial continuum. Coordinate-shuffle permutation tests show this is non-random on the immune-hot face (E14S), with a weaker trend on the tumor-core face (E15S).

What “front” means Permutation test Maps

What “organized as a front” means

Not scattered hypoxic islands. A spatial continuum along an oxygen boundary: normoxic → exiting → persistent → entering → entering-deep. Cells entering hypoxia sit next to cells exiting hypoxia — like a moving front.

Coordinate-shuffle permutation test

Shuffling cell coordinates among a fixed set of tissue positions is equivalent to shuffling dynamics labels on the fixed point cloud / kNN graph. We use n = 2000 permutations and two nulls:

  • Shuffle all coordinates — destroys all structure (too harsh; mixes tumor into immune regions).
  • Shuffle tumor coordinates only — keeps the tumor scaffold; randomly re-paints dynamics onto tumor cells. This is the appropriate null.
