Experimental design

E15S = GFP+ / ImageIT+ — cells sorted as hypoxia-marker positive.
E14S = GFP− / ImageIT− — cells sorted as hypoxia-marker negative.
Marker positivity ≠ ongoing transcriptional hypoxia: many GFP+ cells have reverted (post-hypoxic), which is why not all GFP+ cells are velocity-persistent.

Composition differences between E14S and E15S are partly by design (sort gate), not only different tissue faces. The interesting biology is the continuum within and between gates: fronts, entry≠exit programs, and reverted post-hypoxic cells inside GFP+.

What “organized as a front” means

Not scattered hypoxic islands. A spatial continuum along an oxygen boundary: normoxic → exiting → persistent → entering → entering-deep. In the ImageIT+ pool this is especially natural: marked cells occupy different positions along the same reoxygenation/hypoxia boundary.

Coordinate-shuffle permutation test

Shuffling coordinates among fixed positions ≡ shuffling dynamics labels on the fixed kNN graph. n = 2000 permutations. The appropriate null is shuffle tumor coordinates only (keep the tumor scaffold).
